How we calculate the score

Each opportunity is rated out of 100 using six visible signals. Higher is better in every category; missing information lowers confidence rather than being guessed.

25%Pay & stipend
20%Role clarity
15%Company context
10%Location fit
5%Conversion path
25%Fair-work signals

Worth Score = 25% pay + 20% role clarity + 15% company context + 10% location + 5% conversion + 25% fair-work signals
